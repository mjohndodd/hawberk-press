Welcome to the Hawberk Press family! This portal is your private resource hub — a place to find the tools, guidance, and documentation you need at every stage of your journey with us, from signing your contract through publication and beyond.

## What You'll Find Here

The Author Portal brings together everything Hawberk Press wants you to have at your fingertips: marketing assets, process documentation, guidance on building your online presence, and clear explanations of how we work together. Think of it as the handbook we wish every small press gave its authors — practical, honest, and written with your experience in mind.

## What to Expect from Hawberk Press

Hawberk Press is an independent, digital-first publisher of strange, dark fiction with a speculative edge. We are a small operation, and we mean that as a point of pride. Every author we work with is a deliberate choice. You won't get lost in a roster of hundreds — you'll have a real relationship with the people editing, designing, and championing your work.

We lead with eBooks, invest in audio and physical formats where we can, and pay authors fairly at every step. We don't use AI in our editorial or creative work — your story will be read, considered, and shaped by human hands only. And our commitment to you doesn't end on publication day; we're in this for the long term.
