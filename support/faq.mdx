---

Publishing a story is only the beginning of your relationship with Hawberk Press, and it's natural to have questions along the way. This page collects the things authors ask us most often. If you don't find what you're looking for here, reach out directly — we're a small team and genuinely happy to talk.

<Accordion title="How do I access my marketing assets?">
  Your marketing assets — cover art, promotional images, press copy, and social media graphics — are hosted in the Author Portal under the **Marketing** section. Each title has its own asset folder. If you believe something is missing or you'd like a specific format or size for a particular platform, contact the marketing team and we'll sort it out.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When will I receive my first royalty payment?">
  Royalty payments are issued on the schedule set out in your contract — typically quarterly, following the close of each reporting period. Your first payment will arrive after the first full period in which your title has been on sale. You'll receive a royalty statement alongside every payment, detailing sales by retailer and territory. If the expected date passes without a statement, drop a line to the accounts team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I publish my story elsewhere while it's under contract?">
  That depends on the rights granted in your contract. Most Hawberk Press agreements include an exclusivity period during which your story may not be republished, either in full or in a substantially similar form, through another publisher or on your own channels. Once the exclusivity window has closed, the rights revert in the manner described in your agreement. If you're unsure what your contract permits, the editorial team can walk you through it — please ask before committing to anything elsewhere.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I want to update my author bio?">
  You can update your author bio at any time by submitting the new text via the **Author Profile** section of the Portal. Bios are used across our retailer listings, the Hawberk Press website, and press materials, so once you submit an update we'll apply it across all active channels within a few business days. If you'd like to change your author photo as well, upload a high-resolution version (at least 1000 × 1000 px) alongside the new bio text.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long does the editorial process typically take?">
  The honest answer is: it varies. A short story typically moves through developmental editing, copyediting, and proofreading in four to eight weeks, assuming revisions are returned promptly. Longer works and those requiring more substantial revision will take longer. Your editor will give you a working schedule at the start of the process and keep you informed of any changes. We'd rather take the time to get it right than rush something that deserves care.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use AI tools to help edit or revise my work?">
  Hawberk Press is built on a commitment to human-crafted storytelling — it's central to who we are and what we publish. Using AI tools to generate new prose, substantially rewrite passages, or produce structural suggestions that you then incorporate into the work is not in keeping with that commitment. We want the story to be yours, shaped by your imagination and your craft.

  That said, we recognise that the line between a grammar checker and a generative tool is shifting quickly. If you're considering using any AI-assisted tool during your revision process — even for light editing — please talk to your editor before you do. We're not looking to police your workflow, but we do want to understand what's involved and make sure your work remains authentically yours.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What rights do I retain after publication?">
  You retain the copyright to your work. What you license to Hawberk Press is defined in your contract — typically the right to publish and distribute the story in specified formats and territories for the duration of the agreement. When the contract term ends, or if rights revert earlier under the terms specified, you are free to do as you wish with the work. Your contract document is the definitive reference; the editorial team can clarify any clause you're uncertain about.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get an ISBN or metadata for my story?">
  Hawberk Press assigns ISBNs and manages all bibliographic metadata as part of the publication process — you don't need to source these yourself. Your ISBN and metadata record will be confirmed to you before your title goes on sale. If you need metadata details for a specific purpose (submitting to a library catalogue, for instance), contact the editorial team and we'll provide exactly what you need in the format required.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I serialise or post excerpts of my story on social media?">
  Short excerpts — a paragraph or a striking line — shared for personal promotion are generally welcome and we encourage authors to celebrate their own work. Serialising the story in installments, or posting excerpts that together constitute a significant portion of the work, will likely conflict with your exclusivity terms. Check your contract, and when in doubt ask your editor first. We're not trying to make things difficult; we just want to protect the commercial life of your story while it's under active agreement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if I spot an error in my published story?">
  Tell us immediately. Email the editorial team with the title, the format (eBook, audio, print), the location of the error (chapter, page, or percentage position), and a clear description of what needs correcting. Digital editions can be updated relatively quickly; physical print files take longer to amend but we will address them in the next available print run. Mark your email subject line **URGENT** if the error is significant — a factual mistake, a corrupted section, or a formatting problem that makes the text unreadable.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I update my payment details?">
  Payment details are managed securely through the Author Portal. Navigate to **Account Settings → Payment Information** and follow the steps to update your bank details or payment method. Changes take effect from the next payment cycle. If you encounter any difficulty with the form, contact the accounts team directly — do not send payment details via email.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I submit another story to Hawberk Press?">
  Absolutely, and we'd love to read it. Having an existing relationship with us doesn't guarantee acceptance — every submission is considered on its own merits — but it does mean you're welcome to submit directly to your editor with a note that you're an existing author. Please check the current submission guidelines on the Hawberk Press website before sending, as we occasionally close submissions or adjust our focus. We're always glad to hear from writers we've already worked with.
</Accordion>
