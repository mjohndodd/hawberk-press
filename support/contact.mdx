## Response Times

The Hawberk Press team aims to respond to all author enquiries within **two to three business days**. During busy periods — around submission windows or end-of-quarter royalty processing — it may occasionally take a little longer, but we will always reply. If you haven't heard back within five business days, a gentle follow-up is entirely welcome.

<Tip>
  When you write in, include your story title and the name under which you're contracted in the opening line. It helps us pull up the right records quickly and gets you a faster, more useful reply.
</Tip>

## Informal Queries & Social Media

For lighter, informal questions, you're also welcome to reach out through the Hawberk Press social media profiles. We're active on the channels listed on the main website at [hawberkpress.com](https://hawberkpress.com). Social isn't the right route for anything contract-related or financially sensitive — use the email addresses above for those — but it's a perfectly good way to say hello, flag something small, or share your news.
