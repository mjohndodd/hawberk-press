## How Your Assets Are Delivered

Your assets are made available through three channels depending on their type:

- **This portal** — Core assets such as your cover files and press kit documents are linked directly from the relevant pages in this section. Log in and download at any time.
- **Shared folder** — Your editorial contact will share a dedicated folder (via a private link) containing your complete asset bundle at the time of publication. This folder remains accessible throughout your contract term.
- **By request from your editorial contact** — Custom or one-off items, such as event banners or retailer-specific graphics, are prepared by the Hawberk Press team and sent to you directly by email.

If you are unsure where a particular asset lives, your editorial contact is always the right first port of call.

## Publisher Logo

You're welcome to use the Hawberk Press logo (either the wordmark or the colophon) in contexts where you're representing your work with Hawberk Press.

<Note>
  Please do not use the Hawberk Press logo on merchandise, in book trailers, or in any context that could imply an editorial endorsement of a third-party product or service. When in doubt, ask before you publish.
</Note>

## File Formats Explained

Hawberk Press provides assets in three standard formats. Choosing the right one for the right context keeps your promotional materials looking sharp.

**PNG**
The format of choice for digital use. PNG files preserve fine detail and support transparent backgrounds, making them ideal for social media posts, blog embeds, and anywhere the image sits against a coloured or patterned background.

**JPEG**
Best for photographic images and contexts where file size matters — for example, email newsletters or web pages where load speed is a consideration. Use JPEG versions of your cover art when a PNG would be unnecessarily large.

**PDF**
Use PDF files when sending materials to print, sharing press kits as downloadable documents, or submitting to venues that require print-ready assets. PDFs from Hawberk Press are set to 300 dpi and are suitable for professional printing.

## Requesting Additional Assets

If you need a size or format not included in your standard bundle — an extra-wide banner for a newsletter header, a square crop for a specific platform, or a print-ready version of a graphic — contact your editorial contact by email with the following information:

- The asset you need (e.g., cover image, logo)
- The intended use (e.g., "Facebook event banner")
- The required dimensions or format
- Your deadline, if any

We will do our best to accommodate requests within five working days. During high-volume periods around publication dates, lead times may be slightly longer.

<Tip>
  If you have a recurring need — for example, you post weekly to a newsletter and always need a consistent banner format — mention it early. We can often prepare a template that makes your ongoing promotion much easier.
</Tip>
