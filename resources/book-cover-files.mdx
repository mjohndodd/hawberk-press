Your book cover is the first thing a potential reader sees — and a well-used cover image can do a great deal of promotional work on your behalf. Hawberk Press provides your cover files in multiple sizes and formats so that you always have the right version ready, whether you're posting to Instagram, submitting to a book blog, or sending files to a reviewer.

## Where to Download Your Cover Files

Your complete set of cover files is available in two places:

- **Your shared asset folder** — Your editorial contact will have sent you a private link to your dedicated folder at the time of publication. All cover variants are stored here and can be re-downloaded at any time.
- **This portal** — Once your title is listed in the portal, a download link to your cover bundle appears on your title dashboard. Navigate to **My Titles** and select your book to find it.

If you cannot locate your cover files or your shared folder link has expired, contact your editorial contact and they will resend it promptly.

## Available Sizes and Formats

Your cover bundle includes the following variants:

| File | Resolution | Format | Intended Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cover-hires.png` | 300 dpi | PNG | Print submissions, press kits, high-quality blog features |
| `cover-web.jpg` | 72 dpi | JPEG | Website embeds, email newsletters, general online use |
| `cover-square.png` | 1:1 crop | PNG | Instagram posts, podcast episode art, profile thumbnails |
| `cover-wide.jpg` | 16:9 crop | JPEG | Twitter/X banners, Facebook headers, YouTube thumbnails |
| `cover-ebook.jpg` | Retailer spec | JPEG | Already submitted to retailers; provided for your reference |

<Note>
  The cover art for your title was commissioned and paid for by Hawberk Press. Copyright of the original illustration rests with the artist. However, you hold a full licence to use the cover art freely for all promotional purposes connected to your book — social media, press outreach, events, and beyond. You do not need to seek further permission for standard promotional use.
</Note>

## What You Can and Cannot Do With Your Cover

**You are encouraged to:**
- Share your cover on all personal and professional social media channels
- Include it in your newsletter, author website, and blog
- Submit it alongside review requests, press releases, and interview pitches
- Use it in virtual or in-person event presentations
- Include it in your author press kit

**Please do not:**
- Alter the core design of the cover — including cropping out the title or author name, changing colours, or adding overlaid text — without written approval from your editorial contact
- Use the cover art to imply an endorsement or affiliation with any third party
- Sell or license the cover art independently of the book
- Commission derivative works based on the cover art without written permission from both Hawberk Press and the original illustrator

If you have a specific use case in mind that isn't covered above, ask before you proceed. We are generally supportive of creative promotional uses and prefer a quick conversation to a misunderstanding later.

## Best Practices for Sharing Cover Art

A cover image that has been compressed, cropped incorrectly, or exported at the wrong size reflects on your book — and not kindly. Follow these practices to keep your cover looking its best everywhere it appears.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the correct file for the context">
    Match the variant to the platform. Use the square crop for Instagram, the wide crop for Twitter/X banners, and the high-res PNG when sending to press contacts or print publications. Never upload a web-resolution file where a high-res file is called for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do not re-compress files unnecessarily">
    Avoid opening a JPEG, editing it, and re-saving it as a new JPEG — each save cycle degrades the image quality. If you need to make adjustments for a specific use, work from the PNG source file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check platform size requirements before uploading">
    Platforms change their image dimension requirements periodically. Before a cover reveal or launch post, verify that the variant you are using matches the current specs for that platform to avoid unwanted auto-cropping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your originals archived">
    Store your original downloaded files somewhere safe — a personal cloud folder, a hard drive backup — so you always have clean source files regardless of what happens to the portal or your shared folder link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requesting a New Format or Size

If the bundle does not include a size or format you need, contact your editorial contact with:

- The title and the asset you need
- The exact dimensions or format specification
- The platform or context you're preparing it for
- Any deadline you're working toward

Most format requests can be turned around within five working days.

## Requesting a Cover Correction

If you notice an error on your cover — a typo in the author name, an incorrect series number, a colour inconsistency — raise it with your editorial contact immediately. Include a clear description of the issue and, if possible, a screenshot or annotated image showing exactly what needs correcting.

<Warning>
  Cover corrections after a title has gone live with retailers require coordinated updates across all retail platforms and may take up to two weeks to propagate fully. The sooner you flag an issue, the sooner we can begin the correction process.
</Warning>
