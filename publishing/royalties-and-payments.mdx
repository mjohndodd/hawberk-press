Hawberk Press is committed to paying its authors fairly and as transparently as possible. As a digital-first independent press, eBook sales are the primary revenue stream, and the payment model reflects that. This page explains how the payment structure works, what to expect from your statements, and how to raise questions if something isn't clear. The specifics of your individual agreement — including your rate or split — are set out in your signed contract; this page gives you the framework to understand them.

## Payment Structure

Hawberk Press uses two payment models depending on the nature of the publication. Your contract will specify which applies to your work.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Flat Fee">
    A flat fee is a one-time payment made upon publication (or at a milestone specified in your contract, such as on signing or on delivery of final approved manuscript). Flat fee arrangements are most common for short fiction appearing in themed publications or anthologies. The fee is fixed regardless of subsequent sales performance.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Royalty Split">
    A royalty split entitles you to a percentage of net revenue generated by sales of your work. For eBook sales — the primary format at Hawberk Press — this means a share of the net receipts after platform fees are deducted. Royalty arrangements include regular reporting statements so you can track your earnings over time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Some agreements may combine both structures — for example, an advance against royalties, where the flat payment is recouped from future royalties before further earnings accrue. Your contract will make the structure explicit.

<Info>
  Hawberk Press is an independent press operating with the economics of the independent publishing world. Payment rates are set with the genuine intention of being fair to authors within those constraints, and the press is transparent about what it can and cannot offer. If you have questions about how your rate was arrived at, your editor is the right person to ask.
</Info>

## Payment Schedule

Hawberk Press operates on a **quarterly reporting cycle**. Royalty statements are issued four times per year, covering the preceding quarter's sales data from all distribution platforms. Payment for any balance due is made within **30 days of the statement date**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reporting Quarters" icon="calendar">
    Statements cover January–March, April–June, July–September, and October–December. You'll receive your statement and any payment due within the first month following the end of each quarter.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flat Fee Timing" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    If your agreement specifies a flat fee, payment is made according to the milestone set out in your contract — typically within 30 days of the triggering event (signing, acceptance of final manuscript, or publication date).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Read Your Royalty Statement

Your royalty statement will arrive by email and will also be accessible through the author portal. Each statement includes:

- **Reporting period** — the quarter the statement covers
- **Units sold** — broken down by platform and format where available
- **Gross revenue** — total sales income before platform fees
- **Net revenue** — income after platform fees are deducted (this is the figure your royalty percentage applies to)
- **Royalty earned** — your share of net revenue for the period
- **Running total** — cumulative earnings to date, and any balance carried forward if applicable
- **Payment amount** — the sum being paid with this statement, or a note if earnings fall below the minimum payment threshold for the period

<Accordion title="What if my statement shows zero sales?">
  A zero-sales statement is still a statement — it means no units were sold through tracked channels during that period, and it resets the reporting clock for the next quarter. If you believe sales should have been recorded that aren't showing, contact the editorial team with your story title and the relevant period so they can investigate with the distributor.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the minimum payment threshold?">
  If your earnings for a given quarter fall below a minimum threshold (specified in your contract), the balance will carry forward to the following quarter rather than being paid out immediately. This avoids administrative costs on very small transfers. The carried balance will appear on subsequent statements until it reaches the payment threshold.
</Accordion>

## Tax Information

Authors are responsible for their own tax obligations on income received from Hawberk Press. The press does not withhold tax on behalf of authors.

- Hawberk Press will provide any documentation required by law — including income records or relevant tax forms — upon request and as applicable to your jurisdiction.
- If you are based outside the UK or the jurisdiction of the press, you are responsible for understanding any applicable withholding treaty provisions and for providing any required tax forms (such as a W-8BEN for US-based tax documentation purposes) before payment is made.
- Keep records of all payments received and correspondence relating to your earnings — your own records are your primary reference for tax purposes.

<Tip>
  Keep a dedicated folder — digital or physical — for all payment correspondence from Hawberk Press: statements, payment confirmations, and any adjustments or corrections. This makes tax season considerably simpler and gives you a clear record if any discrepancy ever needs to be resolved.
</Tip>

## Questions About Payment

If you have a question about a specific statement, a payment you were expecting, or the terms of your payment arrangement, the editorial team is your first point of contact.

<Note>
  When raising a payment query, please include your story title, the relevant reporting period or payment date, and a brief description of the issue. This allows the team to locate your records quickly and respond accurately. Most payment questions can be resolved within one business cycle.
</Note>
