Publishing agreements can feel dense and legalistic, but the principles behind a Hawberk Press contract are straightforward: you grant the press what it needs to publish and distribute your work, you retain everything else, and the terms are clearly defined so there are no surprises down the line. This page walks through the key concepts in plain language. It's here to help you come to your contract informed — not to replace the contract itself, which remains the binding document.

<Note>
  This page provides general guidance based on standard Hawberk Press agreements. The specific terms of your publishing relationship are set out in your individual signed contract, which may differ in particular details. Always refer to your own agreement for specifics, and reach out to the editorial team with any questions before and after signing.
</Note>

## What Rights You Grant

When you publish with Hawberk Press, you license — not sell — certain rights to your work. The rights granted are specific and bounded; they do not affect your underlying ownership of the story.

A standard Hawberk Press agreement typically covers:

- **First digital publication rights** — the right to be the first publisher to release your work in eBook format. This is the core of what digital-first publication means: your story debuts here.
- **Non-exclusive rights in some contexts** — depending on the agreement, rights to certain formats or territories may be non-exclusive, meaning you retain the ability to also license those rights elsewhere once the exclusivity window has passed.
- **Distribution rights** — the right to distribute the work through digital retail and library platforms for the duration of the agreement.

Rights are licensed for a defined period and in defined formats. Your contract will specify exactly what has been licensed, in what formats, and for how long.

## Rights You Always Retain

Your copyright never transfers. Hawberk Press publishes under a licence — it does not acquire ownership of your work. Regardless of what you grant in any agreement, you always retain:

## Exclusivity Windows

Most Hawberk Press agreements include a period of exclusivity — typically **six months from the date of first publication** — during which your story is published exclusively through Hawberk Press channels and should not be republished elsewhere in the same format.

After the exclusivity window closes, the agreement typically converts to non-exclusive terms, meaning you are free to also license the work to other publishers, anthologies, or platforms, while Hawberk Press retains the right to continue selling the work through its own channels.

<Info>
  The exclusivity window applies to republication of the work as it stands. It does not prevent you from writing new work, discussing the story publicly, or including excerpts in author portfolios and promotional materials.
</Info>

## Reversion of Rights

Rights revert to you — fully and automatically — when the terms of your agreement are met or when certain conditions apply. Standard reversion provisions include:

- **Expiry of the agreement term** — if your contract specifies a fixed duration, rights revert at the end of that period unless both parties agree to extend.
- **Out-of-print or unavailability** — if the work becomes unavailable for sale through digital channels for a sustained period (the specific threshold will be defined in your contract), you may request reversion in writing.
- **Mutual agreement** — rights can always revert by mutual written agreement between you and Hawberk Press, regardless of the remaining term.

When rights revert, Hawberk Press will confirm the reversion in writing. You are then free to seek publication elsewhere without restriction.
