Publishing a book is rarely a straight line, but it helps to know the terrain before you start walking it. This page gives you a high-level map of the author journey at Hawberk Press — the major stages, what happens at each one, and roughly what you'll need from us (and from yourself) along the way. Each stage links out to more detailed guidance where it exists.

## The Five Stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Offer & Agreement">
    You've received an offer from Hawberk Press — congratulations, and welcome. This stage is about making sure you understand exactly what you're agreeing to before you sign anything.

    Read your contract carefully. We've written it to be as plain-language as possible, but don't hesitate to ask questions — about royalty rates, rights, term length, or anything else that isn't clear. We want you to feel confident in what you're signing, not just relieved to have an offer.

    Once you're satisfied with the terms, sign and return your agreement. That's the starting gun.

    <Tip>
      You're entitled to take time with your contract. A few days of careful reading is entirely reasonable. If you'd like to discuss any clause before signing, email us directly.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Editorial Process">
    With your contract in place, your manuscript enters the editorial stage. You'll be working with a human editor — a careful reader who knows the kind of fiction we publish and is invested in making your story the best version of itself.

    Expect honest feedback. Hawberk Press publishes strange, dark fiction with a speculative edge, and our editorial approach respects the ambition of that work. We're not here to sand off the sharp corners; we're here to help them land with precision.

    This stage typically involves one or more rounds of revision, followed by a final editorial review before the manuscript moves to production.

    <Note>
      All editorial feedback is written by a human reader. No AI tools are used at any point in the editorial process.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pre-Publication">
    Once the manuscript is editorially approved, the pre-publication stage begins. This is where your book takes its final shape.

    You'll be involved in reviewing your author bio, approving key metadata (title, subtitle, series information if applicable), and — where the process allows — giving feedback on cover direction. Copy editing and proofreading happen here too, so expect to see the manuscript again with a different kind of attention on it.

    This is also when marketing preparation begins: assembling assets, drafting launch materials, and thinking through how to introduce your book to readers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch">
    Publication day. Your story goes live on all platforms simultaneously — eBook first, with any additional formats to follow on their own timelines.

    We'll coordinate the launch with you in advance so you know what's going live, where, and when. You'll receive links to your book's listings and any promotional materials prepared for the release.

    The launch is a milestone, not a finish line. Readers find books on their own schedules, and a quiet first week doesn't mean much in the long run.

    <Info>
      Hawberk Press is digital-first: eBooks lead the release. Audio-drama and physical editions, where planned, follow the initial eBook publication.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Post-Publication">
    The relationship doesn't end when the book goes live — this is where the long-term commitment becomes visible.

    You'll have access to royalty tracking so you can follow your earnings in real time. We'll flag promotional opportunities as they arise and work with you on reader engagement where it makes sense. If you have ideas for reaching your audience, bring them to us.

    We're also interested in your next work. Future submissions from Hawberk Press authors are always welcome, and your track record with us matters in how we approach them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
