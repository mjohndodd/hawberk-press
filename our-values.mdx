Every publisher makes choices about what it values, whether or not it says so plainly. At Hawberk Press, we'd rather be explicit. The four pillars below aren't marketing language — they're the convictions that shape how we edit, how we pay, how we support you, and what kind of press we're trying to be. Understanding them will help you know what to expect from us, and what we hope for in return.

## Our Four Pillars

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mind over Machine" icon="brain">
    Every story we publish is wholly the work of a human imagination. We don't use AI at any stage of the editorial or creative process — not for developmental feedback, not for copy editing, not for cover copy. Your voice, your skill, your vision: that's what we're here to support and to publish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fair Rewards" icon="coins">
    We pay authors and contributors fairly and sustainably. We'll always work within our means as a small independent press, but we're equally committed to paying as well as those means allow — and to being transparent about the numbers. You shouldn't have to guess what your work is worth to us.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Long-term Author Support" icon="handshake">
    Authors are the heart of any publisher. Our relationship with you doesn't end when your book goes live — it continues through royalty tracking, promotional opportunities, reader engagement, and future submissions. We're building something together, and that takes time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Digital-first" icon="tablet">
    eBooks come first. That's where we invest our initial publication energy and where our royalty structure is focused. From there, we work to bring stories into audio-drama and physical formats where it makes sense. Leading with digital lets us move thoughtfully and keep our commitments realistic.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What This Means in Practice

These values show up in concrete ways across your experience as a Hawberk Press author.

**On human creativity:** When you receive editorial notes, they come from a person who has read your work carefully — not from a generated summary or an AI-assisted report. The same is true at every stage of production. We chose your story because a human being was moved by it.

**On fair rewards:** Your contract will spell out royalty rates plainly. If you have questions about the numbers, ask — we'd rather over-explain than leave you uncertain about what you're owed.

**On long-term support:** We'll check in with you after publication, not just before it. If your book picks up momentum, we want to help you build on that. If it's slow to find its audience, we'll think through that with you too.

**On digital-first:** Don't read "digital-first" as "digital-only." It means we start where we can do the most good, then expand. Physical and audio editions are genuine goals, not afterthoughts — they just follow the eBook rather than launching alongside it.
